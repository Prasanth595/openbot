### Added

- The home page of openbot.app has a light beam again. It falls onto the app preview, spreads a
  flat sheet of light across the top of the preview, and runs down the corner of the preview. A
  violet fog of soft clouds surrounds it. The light moves toward the pointer.
- The beam has a different colour, width, spread and haze on each visit. When the window is too
  narrow for the beam to pass the text, the page shows no beam. With the reduced motion setting,
  the beam does not move.
- The beam waits until the app preview has loaded, then falls in. The preview appears as the beam
  hits it. On a slow GPU, the beam uses a smaller canvas.
- On a wide window, the beam falls through the Download button in the site header. The Community
  and App buttons in the site header and the "Available on" label in the hero are frosted glass.
  The App button is after the Download button.
