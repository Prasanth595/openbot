### Added

- The Usage panel shows how much of your Gemini weekly limit is left, and when it resets. This
  needs a Google sign-in and Antigravity 1.4.1 or later: install the update that the Gemini row in
  Settings offers. Before, Gemini always showed "Not reported".
- When a saved Gemini API key runs your Gemini agents, the Usage panel says so. A saved key always
  comes before your Google sign-in, so the work uses the key's project and not your Google AI plan.
