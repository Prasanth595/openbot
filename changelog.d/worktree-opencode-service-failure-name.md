### Fixed

- An OpenCode 2.1 turn that failed in OpenCode's local service now says so and tells you to try again or reconnect OpenCode. Before, the chat showed only "Internal error: Internal service failure".
- A provider error that is not tied to a chat, such as a provider that did not start, now names the provider. Before, it showed the provider's text with no name, so you could not tell which provider failed.
